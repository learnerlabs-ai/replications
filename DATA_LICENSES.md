## Benchmarks used to measure the base model

For every benchmark below except GSM8K, no item is redistributed here: only scores and per-subject accuracies are. GSM8K is the exception, because the free-generation arm had to be published as traces for its grading to be checkable. The four `answers/*/*/gsm8k_think_generations.jsonl.gz` files carry, per graded item, the final 300 characters of the prompt — which is verbatim GSM8K question text, including the few-shot exemplar — together with the gold answer and the model's own output. GSM8K is MIT-licensed, which permits this; the notice is in `third_party_licences/GSM8K_LICENSE.txt`. To re-run any of them, use `lm-evaluation-harness`, which fetches each dataset from its own host under its own terms.

## Corpora of the four-domain comparison

Every window that was trained or measured is recorded in `science/plasticity-without-forgetting/data/windows_index.json` — its domain, source, pinned revision, window count and release class. All windows are published beside it as token ids and decoded text. The other three domains and the control are third-party text and keep the source terms listed below; the MIT and CC BY 4.0 licences of this repository do not cover them.

The sources above are known at the level of the dataset and its pinned revision. The record of which article or page each window was cut from was not kept with the training plan, so most windows cannot be attributed to a source document, and for a set with two sources we do not say which one a given window came from. The third-party windows are not published here. `data/PUBLIC_RELEASE_DISPOSITION.json` records that no public-release decision has been made for them, and `tools/check_public_release.py` in the study folder refuses any export that contains one. For domain 1 the basis for release is the government-work status of the source collections; hearing transcripts also record witnesses who are not government employees, and the windows were not reviewed passage by passage. The article quotes one short excerpt of a Yoruba training window taken from the Yoruba Wikipedia article on Al-Kahina, under CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Base model

Learner 1.0 is built on a third-party base model, Qwen3.6-27B, which is used under its own licence from its publisher. No model weights are in this repository.
